# Radar launch film: spec (v2, in the grammar of the Lovio reference films)

GOAL:    a viewer lost in the flood of AI tools feels relief, sees Radar answer a real question,
         and leaves wanting to open raunakpatil.com/radar.
OUTPUT:  HyperFrames, 1920 x 1080, 30 fps. 44 s x 30 = 1320 frames. Music "Polished Hit", 120 BPM.

REFERENCE GRAMMAR (measured from the two Lovio films the author supplied, 53-54 s each):
         no hard cuts at all (scene detection finds none): every seam is a blur dissolve or a zoom;
         two kinds of world: a deep night ground with a big soft colour glow, and real photographs
         with the product placed on devices; thin, centred sentence-case type, one line at a time,
         arriving word by word through a blur, one word tinted; a frosted-glass prompt box that
         types; a rolling list; an anaphora run over changing photos; a blooming 3D mark into
         the logo; something new every ~1 s.

         Type Inter 400 (titles 88-128 px, -0.035em), the tinted word a cream-to-orange gradient.
         Glass: white 10%, 1.5 px white 24% border, 28 px backdrop blur.
         Photos: the author's AI images; the real Radar screens are mapped onto the laptop and
         phone screens with a measured perspective transform.
