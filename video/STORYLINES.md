# Storylines

1. **The noise, then the signal** (built). Device: life before and after. Form: object-to-object
   chain (names -> tabs -> dot -> radar -> search bar -> cards -> phones -> mark).
2. **A day of one maker.** Device: a moment in someone's day ("record a podcast, need clips by
   tonight"). Form: one continuous take across their desk and phone; Radar answers in one ask.
3. **One continuous zoom.** Device: the object is the explanation. Form: an endless zoom through
   the radar's rings, each ring a feature (ask, news, directory, phones), out to the mark.
